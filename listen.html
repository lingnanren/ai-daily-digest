🥉 XAML 中绑定值类型的危险

GPT‑6 Astra — simonwillison.net · 7 小时前 · 🤖 AI / ML

中文摘要

在 XAML 中将值类型（value types）绑定到 UI 元素时，可能会导致身份（identity）丢失。这种绑定方式可能会引发意外的行为，因为值类型是按值传递的，而不是按引用传递，这使得追踪和管理其状态变得困难。

English Summary

Binding to value types in XAML can lead to a loss of identity. This occurs because value types are passed by value, not by reference, making it difficult to track and manage their state, potentially causing unexpected behavior in UI elements.

💡 为什么值得读: 揭示了 XAML 中一个常见的陷阱，即绑定值类型可能导致难以追踪的 UI 问题。

💡 Why read it: Reveals a common pitfall in XAML where binding to value types can lead to hard-to-track UI issues.
