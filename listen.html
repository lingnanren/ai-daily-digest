7. alloca 等函数如何从栈上分配内存？

Two-Factor Authentication Across Package Registries — nesbitt.io · 15 小时前 · ⭐ 23/30

中文摘要

文章解释了像 alloca 这样的函数如何从调用栈上分配内存。这种内存分配方式与传统的堆分配不同，它直接在当前函数的栈帧中预留空间。alloca 的主要优点是分配速度快且自动释放（随函数返回），但缺点是可能导致栈溢出，且分配的内存无法被显式地 free。这是一种在特定场景下，用于临时存储局部变量或缓冲区的高效内存管理技术。

English Summary

This article explains how functions like alloca allocate memory from the call stack. Unlike traditional heap allocation, this method reserves space directly within the current function's stack frame. The primary advantages of alloca are its speed and automatic deallocation (upon function return), but it carries the risk of stack overflow and the allocated memory cannot be explicitly freed. It's an efficient memory management technique for temporary storage of local variables or buffers in specific scenarios.
