中文摘要

科里·多克托罗（Cory Doctorow）在文章中探讨了商业监控公司如何将用户隐私视为一种市场交换，用户以隐私换取服务访问权。他认为这种“市场”存在根本性问题，因为用户在浏览时，服务提供商就能直接获取用户数据，这并非公平的交易。多克托罗将这种现象与“Enshittification”概念联系起来，并提出了“Dickover”作为一种可能的应对或描述方式，强调了在数字时代重新审视计算的“所有权”和“控制权”的重要性。

English Summary

Cory Doctorow critiques commercial surveillance companies' framing of data collection as a market exchange where users trade privacy for service access. He argues this "market" is inherently unfair, as providers can extract data the moment users engage with their services, likening it to a vendor reaching into a customer's pocket. Doctorow connects this to his 'enshittification' concept and introduces 'Dickover' to describe this exploitative dynamic, emphasizing the need to re-evaluate computational ownership and control in the digital age.

💡 为什么值得读: 深入剖析了数字时代用户隐私与商业利益的冲突，并提出了新的概念来描述这种现象。
