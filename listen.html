English Summary

The irrationality exponent μ(x) measures how well a real number x can be approximated by rational numbers; for rational x, μ(x) = 1, and for irrational x, μ(x) ≥ 2. OpenAI has recently published a proof demonstrating that the irrationality exponent of π, μ(π), is equal to 2. This result indicates that π can be well-approximated by rational numbers, aligning with the fact that almost all real numbers have an irrationality exponent of 2.
