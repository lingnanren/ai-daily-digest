中文摘要

Windows 不支持 32 位 ARM 和 MIPS 等其他架构的热补丁，主要是因为这些架构要么在热补丁技术出现之前就已经存在，要么从未真正需要过该功能。热补丁技术（Hot Patching）允许在不重启系统的情况下更新正在运行的软件，这对于服务器和关键系统尤为重要。然而，对于那些生命周期较短或对可用性要求不那么极致的架构，其开发和维护成本可能超过了引入热补丁支持的收益。

English Summary

Windows does not support hot-patching for architectures like 32-bit ARM and MIPS primarily because these architectures either predate the existence of hot-patching technology or never had a significant need for it. Hot Patching allows for updating running software without rebooting the system, which is crucial for servers and critical systems. However, for architectures with shorter lifecycles or less stringent availability requirements, the development and maintenance costs of introducing hot-patching support may outweigh the benefits.

💡 为什么值得读: 深入了解 Windows 操作系统底层设计决策及其历史演变，特别是关于热补丁支持的限制。
