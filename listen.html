4. π 的无理数指数

Irrationality exponent of π — johndcook.com · 8 小时前 · ⭐ 22/30

中文摘要

OpenAI 证明了 π 的无理数指数 μ(π) = 2。无理数指数衡量一个实数被有理数逼近的程度，对于有理数 μ(x) = 1，对于无理数 μ(x) ≥ 2。μ(π) = 2 的结果表明 π 的无理数性质与大多数实数相似，但这一证明本身是重要的数学进展。

English Summary

OpenAI has proven that the irrationality exponent of π is μ(π) = 2. The irrationality exponent measures how well a real number can be approximated by rational numbers; it is 1 for rational numbers and ≥ 2 for irrational numbers. The result μ(π) = 2 indicates π's irrationality is typical, but the proof itself is a significant mathematical advancement.

🏷️ irrationality exponent, pi, mathematics, OpenAI
